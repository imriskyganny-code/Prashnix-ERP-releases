## 🌟 What is Prashnix ERP?
**Prashnix ERP** is an institutional-grade Windows desktop management platform designed specifically for schools, academies, and educational organizations. 

Unlike recurring SaaS platforms that store sensitive student records and financial ledgers on third-party servers, Prashnix ERP operates **100% offline** on your local machine with an embedded SQLite engine and direct Google Drive cloud backup.

---

## 🚀 Key Modules & Capabilities

- 💰 **Live Financial Fee Counter**: Multi-head fee billing, sibling-linked family ledgers, instant A4 vector & POS thermal receipts, and real-time dues reports.
- 📝 **Examination & Assessment Engine**: Automated multi-term marks calculation (Tests 1–3, Half Yearly, Yearly), scholastic/co-scholastic grading, green sheet broadsheets, and printable report cards.
- 🎓 **Student Scholar Register (SR)**: Permanent student profiles, 1-click Transfer Certificate (TC), Character & Bonafide certificate issuance, and bulk Excel import.
- 📊 **Accounting & Double-Entry Cashbook**: Real-time Debit/Credit transaction log, operating expense categorization, vendor billing ledgers, and session balance sheets.
- 👥 **Staff Directory & Monthly Payroll**: Salary matrix calculation, advance tracking, and salary payment vouchers.
- 📦 **Inventory & Asset Tracking**: Item catalog, low-stock warnings, purchase bills, and consumption logs.
- 🪪 **CR-80 PVC ID Card Printing**: 180+ card variations with direct thermal PVC printer support and A4 sheet multi-card imposition.
- 🔄 **Integrated Auto-Updater**: Automated background checks with safe in-place upgrades that never overwrite local databases.

## 💻 System Requirements
- **OS**: Windows 10 or Windows 11 (64-bit)
- **RAM**: 2 GB RAM minimum (4 GB recommended)
- **Storage**: ~150 MB free disk space
- **Printers**: Any standard Laser/Inkjet printer, thermal receipt printer, or PVC card printer
